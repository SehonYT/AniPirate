# AniPirate

> **Discover more. Search less.**

AniPirate is a curated directory built to make discovering useful digital platforms, services, applications, tools, and communities easier.

From anime and manga to movies, TV, games, music, and apps, AniPirate brings everything into one organized place so you can spend less time searching and more time discovering.

---

## ✨ What is AniPirate?

The internet is full of great websites and applications, but finding them can mean going through endless search results, outdated lists, and dozens of open tabs.

**AniPirate is built to change that.**

Every entry in the directory is reviewed before being added, organized into a relevant category, and maintained over time. The goal is simple: create a directory that is actually useful instead of becoming another giant list of random links.

### What makes AniPirate different?

- 🔎 **Hand-Curated** — Entries are reviewed before they are added.
- 🗂️ **Organized** — Everything is grouped into clear categories for easier discovery.
- 🔄 **Maintained** — Entries and links are regularly checked and updated.
- 👥 **Community-Driven** — Users can suggest new entries and report outdated or broken ones.
- 🛡️ **Safety-Focused** — Listings include useful safety information where available.
- 🌐 **Built for Discovery** — Find useful platforms without opening dozens of tabs.

## 🔍 How AniPirate Works

AniPirate follows a straightforward process for keeping the directory useful:

**Discover → Review → Categorize → Publish → Re-check**

### 1. 🔎 Discover

New services can be found by the AniPirate team or suggested by the community.

### 2. 🔬 Review

Before an entry is published, it is manually reviewed to help identify broken, misleading, low-quality, or otherwise unsuitable services.

### 3. 🗂️ Categorize

Approved entries are placed into the category that best matches what they provide.

### 4. ✅ Publish

Once reviewed, the entry is added to the directory with relevant information to help users understand what they are visiting.

### 5. 🔄 Re-check

The web changes constantly. Existing entries may be reviewed again when information becomes outdated, links stop working, or the community reports an issue.

## 🌟 Our Philosophy

### Quality over quantity

A smaller collection of useful, reviewed entries is better than a huge collection of unverified links.

### Context before the click

Users should have useful information about a service before opening an external website.

### Simple discovery

Finding something useful shouldn't require searching through endless pages or keeping fifty tabs open.

### Community matters

A good directory gets better when its users can suggest services, report problems, and share feedback.

### Always improving

AniPirate is an evolving project. Categories, entries, features, and the way the directory works can improve over time.

## 🤝 Contributing

Found something that belongs on AniPirate?

You can suggest a platform or service for review through the AniPirate website.

### Good submissions should:

- Have a clear purpose.
- Provide useful functionality, content, or tools.
- Be reasonably accessible and operational.
- Fit an existing category or have a good reason for a new one.
- Provide enough information for the team to review it.

Submitting an entry does **not** guarantee that it will be published. Each submission is reviewed before being added.

You can also help by reporting:

- 🔗 Broken links
- 📝 Outdated information
- 🗂️ Incorrect categories
- ⚠️ Suspicious or problematic listings
- 🔄 Major changes to existing services

## 🛡️ Safety & Third-Party Services

AniPirate is a directory. Some entries link to websites, applications, or services operated by independent third parties.

Although entries are reviewed before publication, **no external service can be guaranteed to remain safe, reliable, available, or unchanged forever**. A website can change ownership, domains, advertising, content, software, or security practices at any time.

When visiting external services, users should:

- 🔐 Check the domain before entering personal information.
- 📥 Be careful when downloading files or installing applications.
- 🔄 Keep their device, browser, and security software updated.
- 🚫 Avoid sharing sensitive information with unfamiliar services.
- 🛡️ Use appropriate privacy and security protections.
- ⚠️ Report suspicious or compromised listings to AniPirate.

A listing on AniPirate does not mean that AniPirate operates or controls the third-party service.
